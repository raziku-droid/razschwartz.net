Owning the experience for a new LEGO product from the ground up, and building the team to deliver it, is the kind of role I do my best work in. I would love to bring that to LEGO Digital Play.

I have built UX organisations from zero before. At Welltech I built the design, research and writing functions from scratch into a 40+ person team, working directly with the CEO and CPO to set the standards, workflows and quality bar as the products scaled across markets. At Spotify I led new product innovation design and research across mobile, from early concepts to launches like HiFi Lossless, AI Playlist and Wrapped, which reached 400M+ users across 180+ markets.

Trust and safety by design is not new territory for me. I co-authored research on harassment in social VR, published at CSCW in 2019, on how people experience harm in embodied spaces and what that means for platform design and governance. A younger, family audience raises that bar further, and I would build age-appropriate design and safety expertise into the team from the start.

My background is in Human-Computer Interaction research, so I validate decisions through usability testing and user insight rather than opinion, and I partner closely with research teams. I care about clarity and accessibility as much as visual craft, and drove accessibility in Spotify's new product work.

LEGO is the brand I would most like to build for. I would welcome the chance to talk about the product and the team you are building.
