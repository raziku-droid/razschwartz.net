Dear LEGO Digital Play hiring team,

Low floor, high ceiling, wide walls is the right brief for creator tools, and it is the problem I have found most rewarding in my career. I would love to help LEGO Digital Play build the tools the next generation uses to make games, stories and worlds.

At Meta/Oculus I worked on Horizon's world-building and creation tools and on avatars. That work was about the same tension your role describes: making creation feel immediate for someone placing their first object, without capping what an experienced creator can build. I was one of the first design and research leaders on Oculus, helped set the UX principles for Meta's social VR platform, and shipped Rooms, Venues and Workrooms, alongside leading design and insights for Oculus Go and Quest.

My background is in Human-Computer Interaction research, and I lead by testing early. At Spotify I ran new product innovation and built a rolling concept-testing programme that let us validate or drop ideas fast, taking products like HiFi Lossless and AI Playlist from prototype to launch. For creator tools I would apply the same discipline through structured playtesting, with success measured by creation completion, creator progression and the quality of what gets published, not just time spent.

LEGO Digital Play is at its earliest phase, and building from zero is where I do my best work. At Welltech I built the design, research and writing functions from scratch into a 40+ person team, working directly with the CEO and CPO, and led products through regulated markets where privacy and trust were non-negotiable. Designing for children raises that bar further. I would build safety, privacy and developmental expertise into the team from day one and playtest with children and parents throughout.

Constructionist play, where you learn by making, is the idea I believe in most about how people grow. I would welcome the chance to talk about how I could help bring it to life for LEGO.

Best regards,
Raz Schwartz
